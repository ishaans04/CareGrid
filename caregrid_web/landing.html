The response operating system for campuses

Every second, accounted for.

CareGrid turns the first alarm on campus into an owned incident, a dispatched unit into a recorded arrival, and a closed case into proof that care was delivered.

03 / Proof of response

A notification is not a response.

Most alerting tools stop at “sent”. CareGrid keeps every assignment open until the people on the ground record each step, and an incident cannot close until care is on the record.

05 / Roles

One record. Five vantage points.

Each person sees what their part of the response requires, and nothing more. The server enforces every boundary.

CMD

Command

Owns the incident.

  • Queue, evidence and plan
  • Approves dispatch and escalates
  • Closes with a recorded outcome
RSP

Responder

One task. A clear next step.

  • Location, route and instructions
  • Records each milestone
  • Camera evidence withheld
MED

Medical

Ready before arrival.

  • Incoming case and handoff state
  • Confirms team and bay readiness
  • Footage and track IDs withheld
ADM

Administrator

Keeps the campus ready.

  • Readiness suite and fixes
  • Measured after-action metrics
  • Complete audit trail
MBR

Member

Help starts with a signal.

  • Sends an SOS with location
  • Follows the response live
  • Sees only their own requests
